At the 2025 ASCE World Environmental and Water Resources Congress (Anchorage, AK, May 2025), we presented multi-objective optimization and surrogate-assisted modeling of building water systems on high-performance computing (Sabri, Moradi, and Lee), an analysis of New York City drinking water quality complaints from 2010 to 2024, and our NSF curriculum project.
