At the 3rd International Joint Conference on Water Distribution Systems Analysis & Computing and Control in the Water Industry (Paphos, Cyprus, May 2026):

- the iBWS vision paper on autonomous building water systems and digital twins;
- a Battle of the Water Futures entry with Jonathan Keck, on debt-aware water master planning under deep uncertainty;
- a study linking AWWA's State of the Water Industry outlook to water sector financial performance, 2004–2025;
- nationwide patterns of water service line failures, with S. Wohlfahrt and M. Meehan.
