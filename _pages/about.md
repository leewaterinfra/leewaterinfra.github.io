Water in a pipe is never just H₂O. It carries gases, minerals, disinfectant, metals, and life, and it constantly reacts with the pipe that holds it and the people who use it.

Since 2002, I have studied what happens to that water inside the buildings where we live, learn, and heal: how it moves, how it changes, and when it stops being safe.
